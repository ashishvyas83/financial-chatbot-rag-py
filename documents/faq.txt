FIRSTBANK FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I reset my online banking password?
A: Visit firstbank.com, click "Forgot Password", enter your registered email, and follow the link sent to reset your password. For security, links expire after 24 hours.

Q: What are FirstBank's customer service hours?
A: Phone support is available Monday-Friday 8AM-8PM, Saturday 9AM-5PM EST. Online chat is available 24/7. Branch hours vary by location.

Q: How do I report a lost or stolen card?
A: Call our 24/7 emergency line at 1-800-FIRST-BK immediately. We will freeze your card and issue a replacement within 3-5 business days.

Q: What is the daily ATM withdrawal limit?
A: Standard limit is $500/day. Premium account holders get $1,000/day. Limits can be temporarily increased by calling customer service.

Q: How long do wire transfers take?
A: Domestic wire transfers: Same day if initiated before 4PM EST. International wire transfers: 2-5 business days depending on destination country.

Q: Is my money safe with FirstBank?
A: Yes. All deposits are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor. FirstBank maintains an AA credit rating and has been serving customers since 1923.

Q: How do I open a new account?
A: You can open an account online at firstbank.com in 10 minutes, visit any branch, or call customer service. You will need a government ID and Social Security Number.

Q: What credit score do I need for a loan?
A: Requirements vary by loan type. Personal loans require 650+, home loans require 680+, auto loans require 620+. We also consider income and employment history.
