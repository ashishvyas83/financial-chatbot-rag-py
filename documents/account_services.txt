FIRSTBANK ACCOUNT SERVICES

1. Checking Account
- Minimum balance: $500 (waived with direct deposit)
- Monthly fee: $12 (waived if balance maintained)
- ATM access: Free at 50,000+ FirstBank ATMs nationwide
- Overdraft protection: Available, $35 fee per incident
- Online banking: Available 24/7
- Mobile check deposit: Available

2. Savings Account
- Minimum balance: $100 to open
- Interest rate: 4.5% APY (Annual Percentage Yield)
- Monthly fee: No fee
- Withdrawal limit: 6 per month
- FDIC insured up to $250,000

3. Fixed Deposit (CD)
- Minimum deposit: $1,000
- Terms: 3 months to 5 years
- Interest rates:
  * 3 months: 4.8% APY
  * 6 months: 5.0% APY
  * 1 year: 5.2% APY
  * 2 years: 5.0% APY
  * 5 years: 4.8% APY
- Early withdrawal penalty: 90 days interest

4. Account Balance & Transactions
- Real-time balance updates
- Transaction history: Up to 7 years
- Statement frequency: Monthly (paper or electronic)
- Dispute resolution: Within 10 business days
